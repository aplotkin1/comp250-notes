Vim 03 - AI and Effort

# Title

Struggling Can Be Part of Learning

# Quotation

“When the effort is the point.”

# Citation

Mollick, Ethan. “15 Times to Use AI, and 5 Not to.”
One Useful Thing, December 9, 2024.
https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/15-times-to-use-ai-and-5-not-to.

# Point Locator

“5 Times Not to Use AI,” no. 4.

# Commentary

This quote stood out to me because sometimes the difficult
part of an assignment is what actually helps us learn. Using
AI to avoid every difficult step could prevent students from
developing their own skills. Making mistakes and trying again
can lead to a better understanding of a subject. AI should
therefore help with the learning process without removing
all of the effort required to learn.

# Keywords

artificial intelligence, learning, effort, problem solving
