Vim 07 - Zotero and Citations

# Title

Zotero Connects Research and Writing

# Quotation

“To add a citation from your Zotero library, click on an area
in your Word document where you want to add the citation.”

# Citation

Bradburn, Steven. “How To Use Zotero (A Complete Beginner’s
Guide).” YouTube video, 12:40. October 13, 2021.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JG7Uq_JFDzE.

# Point Locator

09:07

# Commentary

This section is important because collecting sources is only
one part of doing research. Students eventually need to use
those sources correctly in their writing and provide
citations. Zotero's connection with a word processor can make
the citation process easier and more organized. This can save
time while also helping students keep track of where the
information in their papers came from.

# Keywords

Zotero, citations, writing, references
