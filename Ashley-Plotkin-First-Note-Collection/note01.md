Vim 01 - AI and Learning

# Title

AI Should Not Replace Learning

# Quotation

“To learn something new, you are going to have to do the
reading and thinking yourself.”

# Citation

Mollick, Ethan. “15 Times to Use AI, and 5 Not to.”
One Useful Thing, December 9, 2024.
https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/15-times-to-use-ai-and-5-not-to.

# Point Locator

“5 Times Not to Use AI,” no. 1.

# Commentary

This quote shows that AI should not completely replace the
learning process. If students let AI do all of their reading
and thinking, they might finish their work without actually
understanding the material. AI can still be helpful when it
is used to explain difficult ideas or help someone think
through a problem. This makes me think that students need to
find a balance between using AI for assistance and doing the
work needed to actually learn.

# Keywords

artificial intelligence, learning, critical thinking, education
