### 3. Generative AI Research Proposal

**[View Research Proposal](Ashley-Plotkin-Research-Proposal/)**

**Research Topic:** Generative AI in Education: Balancing Technology and Student Development

**Research Question:** Should generative AI be allowed in higher education but restricted in K–8 education to protect students' learning and development?

This research examines the role of generative AI in education, focusing on how AI affects learning, critical thinking, creativity, and independent skill development.

My position is that college students should be allowed to use generative AI under clear university guidelines, while younger students should face greater restrictions to protect the development of foundational academic skills.

The project includes a research proposal, an explanation of my writing process, and eight research sources.
