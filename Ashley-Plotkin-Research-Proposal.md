## Part 1: Topic, Question, and Problem

My research topic is the impact of generative artificial intelligence on education, specifically focusing on fairness, student privacy, and algorithmic tracking. I want to explore how generative AI can be used to improve education while also creating new challenges for students and schools. My main research question is: How does generative AI affect fairness, student privacy, and the way schools track student information? This question is important because generative AI can help students learn and make education more accessible, but it can also raise concerns about unequal access to technology, the collection of personal information, and student monitoring. By researching both the benefits and risks, I hope to better understand how generative AI is changing education and what schools can do to protect students while using these technologies responsibly.
