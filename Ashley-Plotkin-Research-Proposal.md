## Part 1: Topic, Question, and Problem

My research topic is the use of generative artificial intelligence in education, specifically focusing on how it affects students at different educational levels. Generative AI tools, such as ChatGPT, can help students understand difficult concepts, improve their work, and develop problem-solving skills. However, these tools also raise concerns about students becoming too dependent on technology instead of developing essential academic skills independently. My main research question is: Should generative AI be allowed in higher education but restricted in K–8 education to protect students' learning and development? I believe that college students should be allowed to use generative AI as long as universities establish clear rules about when and how it can be used. However, I think K–8 students should not use generative AI because they are still developing important skills such as reading, writing, speaking, critical thinking, and problem-solving. Research on children's creative collaboration has identified both benefits and concerns about AI dominating children's decisions, which makes it important to examine these risks further. I also want to investigate existing government laws and educational policies, including Illinois Public Act 104-0399, to understand how schools currently regulate AI use. I believe schools should establish different policies based on students' educational levels, with greater restrictions for younger students. By researching this issue, I hope to determine how schools can balance the educational benefits of generative AI with the need to protect students' independent learning and development.
