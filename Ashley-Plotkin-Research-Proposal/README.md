# Generative AI in Education: Balancing Technology and Student Development

**Author:** Ashley Plotkin  
**Course:** COMP 250  
**University:** Loyola University Chicago

## Project Overview

This research project explores the use of generative artificial intelligence in education, focusing on the differences between K–8 and higher education. The goal is to examine how generative AI affects student learning, critical thinking, and independent skill development.

## Research Question

Should generative AI be allowed in higher education but restricted in K–8 education to protect students' learning and development?

## Research Position

I believe that college students should be allowed to use generative AI as long as universities establish clear guidelines about when and how it can be used.

However, I believe K–8 students should not use generative AI because they are still developing essential skills such as reading, writing, speaking, critical thinking, and problem-solving.

## Research Focus

This project will examine:

- The benefits of generative AI in higher education.
- The potential risks of AI overreliance among younger students.
- The effects of generative AI on creativity and independent learning.
- Government laws and educational policies regulating AI use.
- The importance of age-appropriate AI guidelines.
