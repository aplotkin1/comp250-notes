
# Note 1: Limited Research on Younger Students

**Source:** Alfarwan, Abdullah. 2025. “Generative AI Use in K-12 Education: A Systematic Review.” *Frontiers in Education* 10: 1647573. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2025.1647573.

**Quotation:**

“Middle schools have a good representation, while about two papers each focused on all students regardless of their grade bands, primary, elementary, and secondary schools.” (Alfarwan 2025, sec. 3.1.2)

**Commentary:**

I find it concerning that there is less research on elementary school students and generative AI compared to older students. Younger children are still developing important skills like reading, writing, critical thinking, and problem-solving. I believe elementary students should have stronger AI restrictions than high school students because they need opportunities to develop these skills independently before relying on technology. I also wonder whether schools are creating policies for younger students based on research that mainly focuses on older students. Since children develop at different stages, I don't think the same AI rules should apply to every grade level.
