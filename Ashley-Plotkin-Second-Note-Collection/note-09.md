# Note 9: The Importance of Verifying AI-Generated Information

**Source:** McGrath, Cormac, Alexandra Farazouli, and Teresa Cerratto-Pargman. 2025. “Generative AI Chatbots in Higher Education: A Review of an Emerging Research Area.” *Higher Education* 89: 1533–1549. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10734-024-01288-w.

**Quotation:**

“the process requires continuous validation of the output of the chatbot-generated material.” (McGrath, Farazouli, and Cerratto-Pargman 2025, 1542)

**Commentary:**

I believe college students should always double-check important information generated by AI, especially when using it for research papers, assignments, and coding projects. ChatGPT can provide helpful explanations and suggestions, but that does not mean everything it produces is accurate. For example, when using AI to help with programming, students should test the code themselves and make sure they understand how it works instead of assuming it is correct. The same applies to research papers because AI might provide incorrect information or unreliable sources. I think verifying AI-generated material is an important part of using the technology responsibly. Students should still be responsible for the accuracy and quality of their own work. This supports my argument that colleges should teach students how to evaluate AI responses rather than allowing them to rely on the technology without questioning it.
