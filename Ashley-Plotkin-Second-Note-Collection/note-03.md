# Note 3: Protecting Foundational Learning Before AI Use

**Source:** Alfarwan, Abdullah. 2025. “Generative AI Use in K-12 Education: A Systematic Review.” *Frontiers in Education* 10: 1647573. https://doi.org/10.3389/feduc.2025.1647573.

**Quotation:**

“The author opines that GenAI facilitates teaching and learning and can be a powerful support for teachers, but agency and decision-making must remain with the teacher.” (Alfarwan 2025, sec. 4.3)

**Commentary:**

I understand why teachers might find generative AI useful for creating lessons and helping students understand difficult topics. However, I don't think those benefits necessarily mean elementary students should use AI themselves. I believe younger students need to develop their reading, writing, and problem-solving skills through their own efforts and guidance from their teachers. If students become accustomed to receiving immediate answers from AI at an early age, I worry that they might not develop the patience or confidence to work through difficult problems independently. I also think there is an important difference between teachers using AI to prepare educational materials and students using AI to complete assignments. Schools could allow teachers to use these tools while restricting direct student access during elementary education.
